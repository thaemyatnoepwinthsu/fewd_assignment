About Bean Boutique

At Bean Boutique, we believe every cup of coffee tells a journey of craftsmanship, passion, and sustainable farming.

Founded in 2018, our mission has been to connect local coffee lovers with world-class ethically sourced coffee beans. From single-origin micro-lots to our signature house espresso blends, we craft an elevated experience for every palate.

  • 100% Fair Trade & Direct Trade Sourced
  • Small Batch Artisanal Roasting
  • Certified Master Baristas
